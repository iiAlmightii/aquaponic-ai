Aquaponics AI Survey Questions
==============================

1. What is the name of your farm or project?
2. Where is your farm located? (City, State)
3. What is the total area of your aquaponic system in square metres?
4. How would you describe your experience with aquaponics?
5. What type of aquaponic system are you running?
6. Which fish species are you cultivating?
7. How many fish are currently in your system?
8. What is the total volume of your fish tank(s)?
9. What is the current average weight of your fish?
10. How much feed do you provide per day?
11. What is your expected harvest cycle for fish?
12. What crops are you growing in your system?
13. What area is dedicated to crop production?
14. What is your expected monthly crop yield?
15. Do you have automated IoT water quality sensors installed?
16. What is your typical water pH level? (Asked only when IoT sensors are not installed)
17. What is the typical water temperature in your system?
18. What was your total infrastructure investment (tanks, pipes, grow beds)?
19. What was the cost of equipment (pumps, lights, sensors, aerators)?
20. What did you spend on initial fish fingerlings and plant seedlings?
21. What is your monthly fish feed expenditure?
22. What is your monthly labour cost (salaries, wages)?
23. What are your monthly utility costs (electricity, water)?
24. What are your monthly maintenance costs (repairs, consumables, water treatment chemicals)?
25. What is your expected monthly revenue from fish sales?
26. What is your expected monthly revenue from crop sales?
27. Over how many months would you like the financial plan to project?
28. What is your primary goal for this aquaponic operation?
29. What is your biggest operational challenge right now?

Land Survey Questions
=====================

Base questions:
1. Enter total land area in square meters.
2. Monthly water cost in rupees (borewell/tanker/canal charges).
3. Electricity usage per month in units (for pumps, motors, lighting).
4. Monthly fertilizer cost in rupees (all types: urea, DAP, organic, etc.).
5. Number of workers.
6. Monthly salary per worker in rupees.
7. Monthly land rent in rupees. Say zero if owned.
8. Total machine cost in rupees.
9. Initial farm setup cost in rupees.
10. Cost of seeds per crop cycle in rupees.
11. Electricity cost per unit in rupees.
12. Monthly maintenance cost in rupees.
13. Estimated post-harvest spoilage percentage. Out of 100 kg harvested, how many kg usually get wasted before sale? Example answer: 8 (means 8%).
14. Temporary seasonal labor cost per month in rupees.
15. Pesticide cost per month in rupees.
16. Fuel cost per month in rupees.
17. Transport cost to market per month in rupees.
18. State where the farm operates.
19. District where the farm operates.
20. Primary mandi or market name. Say unknown if not sure.
21. Market type.
22. Demand level.

Crop-collection questions (repeated per crop):
23. Tell crop name.
24. For <crop_name>, how many times do you grow it in one year?
25. For <crop_name>, months from planting to harvest.
26. For <crop_name>, expected total yield per harvest in kilograms.
27. Do you want to add another crop?

Validation/confirmation question used during voice flow:
28. You said <captured_answer>. Is that correct?
